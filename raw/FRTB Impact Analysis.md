# FRTB Impact Analysis & Industry Studies

## 1. Capital Impact Estimates

| Source | Estimated SA Capital Increase vs Basel 2.5 | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| EBA QIS (various) | +40–70% (average trading book) | Significant variation by asset class |
| Industry consensus | +50–60% for diversified banks | Higher for rates/credit-heavy books |
| GIRR-heavy banks | +30–50% | Benefit from specified currency discount |
| Equity-heavy banks | +60–100% | High equity risk weights (30–70%) |
| Exotic/structured desks | +100%+ | RRAO adds 1% of notional |

## 2. EBA 2024 Benchmarking — Key Findings

### FRTB-ASA (Alternative Standardised Approach)
- First dedicated EBA report on the FRTB-ASA (2024), focusing on the Sensitivities-Based Method
- **SbM Own Funds Requirements show significantly lower dispersion** than traditional IMA risk measures (VaR/sVaR) — indicating improved consistency
- **Reporting quality has improved** since initial FRTB reporting began

### Areas of Inconsistency
- Default Risk Charge (DRC) application
- Residual Risk Add-On (RRAO) scoping — some banks incorrectly flag simple products as complex
- Risk factor bucketing — particularly for multi-underlying products

### Data Quality as Capital Driver
- Incorrectly classifying "simple" products as complex → unnecessary RRAO charges (1% of notional)
- Errors in sensitivity bucketing → incorrect risk weights applied
- Data quality directly impacts capital consumption

## 3. Strategic Considerations

### Output Floor Impact
- With the Basel III Output Floor (72.5% of SA RWA), the SA has moved from a reporting fallback to a **potentially binding capital constraint**
- Banks approved for IMA must still compute SA as a floor
- This makes SA accuracy and optimisation commercially important, not just a compliance exercise

### Global Divergence (as of April 2026)
| Jurisdiction | FRTB Capital Status | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Basel Committee | Effective 1 Jan 2023 | — |
| EU (CRR3) | Reporting from 2025; binding deferred to **2027** | Output floor phased to 2030 |
| UK (PRA) | Binding from **1 Jan 2027** | Aligned with EU delay |
| US (Basel III Endgame) | Significantly delayed / uncertain | Political headwinds |
| Japan | Implemented 2024 | — |
| Singapore (MAS) | Implemented 2023 | — |
| Hong Kong (HKMA) | Implemented 2024 | — |

### Proportionality
- Continued advocacy for proportionate implementation (AFME, ISDA)
- Concerns about impact on market-making capacity and liquidity
- Simplified SA provides relief for smaller firms
