# CRR3 (Regulation 2024/1623) — Market Risk / FRTB Summary

## 1. Overview

Regulation (EU) 2024/1623 (CRR3) finalises the implementation of the Fundamental Review of the Trading Book (FRTB) in the European Union. While CRR2 introduced FRTB as reporting-only, CRR3 transforms it into **binding Pillar 1 capital requirements**.

## 2. Key Dates

| Milestone | Date |
|---|---|
| CRR3 published in Official Journal | June 2024 |
| CRR3 enters into force | 1 January 2025 |
| FRTB binding capital requirements | **Deferred to 1 January 2027** (Article 461a) |
| IMA go-live | Phased: 2027–2029 |
| Output floor fully phased in (72.5%) | 2030 |

> **Note**: The original CRR3 text set the FRTB application date at Jan 2025/2026, but the European Commission exercised Article 461a to defer to **1 January 2027** to preserve level playing field with non-EU jurisdictions (US, UK also delayed).

## 3. Calculation Approaches under CRR3

### Alternative Standardised Approach (A-SA)
- Principal approach for market risk capital
- Incorporates the Sensitivities-Based Method (SbM), Default Risk Charge (DRC), and Residual Risk Add-On (RRAO)
- More granular risk-weighting plus qualitative governance/documentation requirements

### Alternative Internal Model Approach (A-IMA)
- Stringent desk-level approval required
- Expected Shortfall replaces VaR as core risk measure
- Rigorous P&L attribution test and risk factor eligibility test
- EBA to issue binding RTS on approval criteria

### Simplified Standardised Approach
- Available for banks with smaller trading exposures (proportionality thresholds)

## 4. Key Divergences from Basel

| Aspect | Basel | CRR3 |
|---|---|---|
| Application date | 1 Jan 2023 | **1 Jan 2027** (deferred) |
| Transitional multipliers | Not in Basel text | EC may set multipliers to neutralise capital impact for 3 years (until 2029) |
| Output floor | 72.5% of SA (immediate) | Phased in to 72.5% by 2030 |
| Specified FX pairs | 19 USD pairs | EU may add EUR pairs via EBA RTS |
| Specified GIRR currencies | 7 + domestic | EUR automatically included for all EU banks |
| Sovereign DRC | National discretion (zero RW) | Broad zero RW for EUR-denominated EU sovereign debt |
| Simplified SA eligibility | At national discretion | Specific proportionality thresholds |
| Disclosure requirements | From 2023 | Deferred to align with 2027 application date |

## 5. EBA Technical Standards (Mandated)

CRR3 mandates EBA to develop binding technical standards (RTS/ITS) on:
1. Risk factor eligibility test specifics
2. P&L attribution test metrics and thresholds
3. Definition of specified currency pairs (including potential EUR pairs)
4. Look-through approach for funds
5. Criteria for CTP inclusion
6. Desk-level IMA approval criteria

## 6. Transitional Provisions

- **Output Floor**: Phases in from 50% (2025) to 72.5% (2030)
- **DRC securitisation**: Transitional provisions for banking book equivalence mapping
- **Transitional multipliers**: EC consulted on temporary ceilings to neutralise capital volatility upon FRTB go-live
