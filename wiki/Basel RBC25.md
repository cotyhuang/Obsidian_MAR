# Basel RBC25 — Boundary Between the Banking Book and the Trading Book

> [!note] Framework Reference
> **Full title**: RBC25 — Boundary between the banking book and the trading book
> **Source**: [Basel Framework](https://www.bis.org/basel_framework/chapter/RBC/25.htm)
> **MAR reference**: [MAR20](https://www.bis.org/basel_framework/chapter/MAR/20.htm)

---

## 1. Purpose

RBC25 establishes the **global standard** for defining which positions belong in a bank's trading book versus its banking book. This boundary is critical because it determines which capital regime applies:

- **Trading book** → market risk capital (MAR)
- **Banking book** → credit risk capital (CRE)

## 2. Trading Book (RBC 25.1–25.7)

Positions must be assigned to the trading book if they are held with **[[Trading Intent]]**, which includes:

- Positions for **short-term resale**
- Positions intended to benefit from **actual or expected short-term price differences**
- Positions that **hedge other trading book positions**

### Key Requirements
- Regular and accurate **marking-to-market** or marking-to-model
- Active and credible **risk management** of positions
- Clear evidence of **trading intent** at inception

## 3. Banking Book (RBC 25.7–25.10)

All positions **not** assigned to the trading book default to the banking book. Typical banking book items include:

- Loans and advances
- Held-to-maturity securities
- Strategic equity investments
- Liabilities (deposits, issued debt)

## 4. Reclassification Rules

> [!warning] Strict Controls
> Reclassification between books is subject to **supervisory approval** and is intended to be rare. Banks must demonstrate that the move is not motivated by capital reduction.

- Transfers require documented rationale and senior management sign-off (RBC 25.9)
- Capital impact of the transfer must be disclosed
- Once moved, positions cannot easily be moved back

## 5. Structural FX Positions (RBC 25.5)

Certain [[Structural FX Positions]] may be **excluded** from the trading book if:

- They are held to hedge the bank's capital ratio against FX movements
- They meet specific criteria defined by the supervisor

## 6. Netting (RBC 25.6)

Netting of positions across books is allowed only if:

- Quick repatriation of profits is feasible
- Risk transfers between books are transparent and documented
