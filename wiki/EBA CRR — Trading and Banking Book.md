# EBA CRR — Trading and Banking Book

> [!note] Framework Reference
> **Regulation**: Capital Requirements Regulation (CRR / CRR3)
> **Key Articles**: 4(1)(85–86), 102, 103, 104, 104a, 104b
> **Source**: [EBA Interactive Single Rulebook](https://www.eba.europa.eu/regulation-and-policy/single-rulebook/interactive-single-rulebook/12674)

---

## 1. Overview

The EBA CRR transposes the Basel [[FRTB Overview|FRTB]] boundary rules into EU law, adapting them for European-specific conditions. While broadly aligned with [[Basel RBC25]], the CRR introduces:

- More **operational detail** and governance requirements
- **Regional exemptions** (e.g., sovereign exposures, promotional loans)
- A **phased implementation** timeline

## 2. Key Articles

### Article 4(1)(85-86) — Definitions

Defines **positions held with [[Trading Intent]]**:

> **(a)** proprietary positions and positions arising from client servicing and market making
> **(b)** positions intended to be resold short term
> **(c)** positions intended to benefit from actual or expected short-term price differences between buying and selling prices or from other price or interest rate variations

### Article 102 — Trading Book Requirement

Establishes the general obligation for institutions to assign positions to the trading book or banking book.

### Article 103 — Group Netting

Provides flexibility for **consolidated group trading books** but adds restrictions on group-level netting to prevent artificial capital reduction.

### Article 104 — Trading Book Definition

Defines the criteria for inclusion in the trading book:
- [[Trading Intent]]
- Hedging of trading book positions
- Liquidity and valuation frequency requirements

### Article 104a — Banking Book Exclusions

Specifies instruments that remain in the **banking book**:
- Sovereign exposures under certain conditions
- Promotional loans
- EU-specific exemptions

### Article 104b — Governance & Boundary Management

> [!important] Key EU Addition
> Article 104b goes beyond Basel by mandating specific **trading desk structures**, trading limits, and supervisory reporting requirements.

- **(1-2)**: Liquidity and valuation criteria for trading book assignment
- **(3)**: Boundary rules for moving positions between books — requires detailed documentation
- **(4)**: Treatment of [[Structural FX Positions]] tied to trading desks

---

## 3. EU-Specific Adaptations

| Aspect | Basel RBC25 | EBA CRR |
|---|---|---|
| Implementation approach | Global, uniform | Phased, proportional |
| Small bank treatment | Same rules apply | Reduced operational burden |
| Sovereign exposures | No special treatment | Specific exemptions (Art. 104a) |
| [[Internal Risk Transfers]] | Strict controls | More flexibility |
